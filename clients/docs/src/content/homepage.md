**Your personal digital assistant.**

Cadet is the personal assistant that lives wherever you need them. They have their own identity, acting on the world thier own email, their own accounts, their own presence, all set up in minutes, not hours. They work for you, but they **aren't** you. For more on what we believe, who we're building for, and what we refuse to compromise on, check out [the Cadet Constitution](/docs/constitution).
