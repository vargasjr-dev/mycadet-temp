**Your personal AI assistant. For real this time.**

Cadet is a personal AI assistant that lives in the secure Cadet Cloud, has its own identity, and actually does things in the world — its own email, its own accounts, its own presence, set up in minutes, not hours. It works for you, but it isn't you. What we believe, who we're building for, and what we refuse to compromise on: [the Cadet Constitution](/docs/constitution).
