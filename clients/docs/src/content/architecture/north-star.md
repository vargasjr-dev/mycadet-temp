Cadet's north star is a two-machine architecture: one hardened core that thinks, remembers, schedules, and holds every key — and one disposable workbench where your assistant experiments, holding nothing precious.

The worst thing that can happen inside the workbench is losing the workbench. This page will carry the full north-star narrative and an interactive diagram; for now, the path there lives on the [roadmap](/docs/roadmap).
